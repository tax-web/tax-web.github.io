GMClient - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27.11.2026

1. Who is responsible

GMClient is operated by the operator of GMClient, Vienna, Austria (the "operator"). Contact for all privacy questions: gmclient.privacy@gmail.com

2. Scope

This policy applies to the GMClient application, which connects the operator's own email accounts to the AI assistant Claude (provided by Anthropic). GMClient is for the operator's personal use only and is not offered to other users.

3. Google user data accessed

When an account holder signs in with Google and grants consent, GMClient requests:

  • Gmail read-only access (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): to search messages and read their content, headers, labels and attachment metadata.
  • Basic profile information (scopes openid, email, profile): the account's email address and name, used only to identify which account is connected.

GMClient does not request permission to send, delete or modify email, and does not access contacts, calendar, files or any other Google service.

4. How the data is used

  • Email data is retrieved only when the account holder asks a question in Claude that requires it (for example "find the invoice from supplier X").
  • The retrieved messages are passed to Claude solely to answer that question for the account holder.
  • Data is not used for advertising, profiling, market research or to train AI models by the operator.
  • Data is not sold, rented or otherwise transferred to third parties.

5. Sharing and processors

Google user data is shared only as needed to provide the function described above:

  • Google Cloud (Google LLC / Google Ireland): hosts the GMClient server and stores access tokens.
  • Anthropic (provider of Claude): receives the email content that the account holder asks Claude to search or read, under the account holder's own Anthropic account and Anthropic's privacy terms.

No other party receives Google user data. Data may be disclosed only if required by law.

6. Storage and retention

  • GMClient does not store email content. Messages are fetched from Gmail at the time of the request and not kept by the server.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a private, access-restricted Google Cloud Storage bucket in the EU (region europe-west1), until access is revoked or the account is disconnected.
  • Server logs contain technical information (time, request type, errors) and are kept for up to 30 days.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS/TLS. Tokens and secrets are stored in access-restricted Google Cloud services (Cloud Storage, Secret Manager). Only the operator has administrative access.

8. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or by disconnecting GMClient in Claude. Stored tokens for that account are then deleted. Deletion can also be requested by email to the contact address above.

9. Google API Services User Data Policy

GMClient's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

10. Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, www.dsb.gv.at).

11. Children

GMClient is not directed at children and is not used by them.

12. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
