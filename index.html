GMClient

GMClient is a private, non-commercial application. It allows its operator to search and read his own email accounts from the AI assistant Claude (by Anthropic), through a server he operates in his own Google Cloud project.

What GMClient does

  • Searches messages in the operator's own email accounts when he asks Claude a question.
  • Reads the content of matching messages to answer that question.
  • Uses read-only access. GMClient cannot send, delete or change any email.

Who can use it

GMClient is used only by its operator with his own accounts. It is not offered, sold or advertised to anyone else.
